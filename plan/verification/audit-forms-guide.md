# Audit forms: how to use the preview

16 October 2026. Synthetic browser-local preview; changes are visible within this browser's saved sample data.

1. Open an audit plan. Use **Edit** to select its lead and several auditors. In **Checklists and questionnaires**, select **Add checklist** or **Add questionnaire** and give the form a name.
2. Choose its owner and select collaborators from the plan team. **View** permits reading; **Edit** permits changes subject to the person's existing role and question assignment. The lead coordinates all forms. An approver also receives review access. Adding a person to the audit team alone does not share a newly created form with them.
3. Optionally configure **Before use** and **After completion**. **All** requires everyone, **Any** requires one reviewer, and **Sequential** follows the displayed selection order. Choose reviewers other than the owner and people who prepared/completed the work. Leave **None** when no approval is needed.
4. Questions and choices edit inline and save automatically. Use the question's settings for answer type, respondent, responsible auditor, evidence, conditions and assessment requirements. In a questionnaire, newly added questions do not require a separate auditor assessment by default; enable one where needed.
5. If configured, select **Submit for preparation approval**. Assigned reviewers open that form and approve or **Return for changes** with a reason. Content is locked during review and after approval. The lead selects **Mark checklist ready** after approval. Released questions can change only through **Start checklist revision**. Each form has its own version history.
6. Open **Responses and assessments** or **Audit questionnaires**, select the form and assign its auditee questions. Respondents save drafts, submit immutable answer versions and reply to clarification through their assigned questionnaire. Other respondents' answers stay separate. Existing invitation previews remain explicitly unverified.
7. Open the form's **Responses and assessments** / **Conduct audit**. Select the form/version, record assigned auditor assessments, link and verify evidence, resolve clarification and link nonconformities to corrective actions. Completion lists the remaining requirements. Hidden conditional questions are not counted as outstanding work.
8. Select **Complete form**, or **Submit completed form for approval** if configured. Pending review/completion locks responses and assessments. Authorized owners/leads can reopen with a reason; prior decisions and assessment/response history stay recorded. Review comments survive navigation/reload in the same tab until submitted or cleared.
9. Use the form row's menu for **Form settings** on a draft or **Withdraw form** with a reason. Withdrawn forms leave active work and remain in **Withdrawn** for authorized history review. Removing a collaborator in the current draft also removes access to the form's historical versions.
10. Select **Copy to another process**, choose destination/date/qualified lead/team and the forms to reuse. The destination gets independent draft questions and fresh IDs. Review destination clauses, responsible auditors, evidence, collaborators and both approval routes. Source answers, assignments, findings, decisions and access grants are not copied. A copied section with no applicable destination clause must be corrected before release.

Cross-device sharing and verified invitations require the shared backend. Audit reports/consolidation are still separate work; completion here does not issue a report.
