# ADR-026: Usable audit authoring and complete audit records

16 October 2026. Danica reported that questionnaires remained uneditable and the audit flow lacked an understandable release, response, finding and completion path. This supersedes ADR-025’s non-team quality-preparer handoff and its deferral of report consolidation in the browser preview.

## Decision

An authorized quality manager or administrator may explicitly own and prepare an audit form without becoming an assessing auditor. Creation defaults to the creator when eligible. Taking ownership of an existing draft is a deliberate, reasoned action with history. Oversight alone does not silently grant ownership. Selected team collaborators retain their existing View/Edit grants. Only an assigned, independently qualified auditor conducts assessments; only the lead releases forms and issues reports.

Questions and choices edit inline and save immediately. A preparer who is not the lead can ask the lead to mark a validated form ready. The lead receives a link to that exact form; configured preparation approval must pass before release. Released content is immutable. A preparer can request a reasoned revision, which the lead starts as a new numbered draft owned by the preparer. Original questions, assignments and submissions remain on their original versions. Readiness, response collection, independent assessment, completion approval and report issuance are distinct actions.

The audit overview presents the next required action and links to forms, questionnaire assignments, Results, findings and the Report. Results separates respondent answers, independent assessments and findings and can select an exact historical form version. Respondents see their own records; team members see permitted forms. Completion requires submitted responses, required assessments and evidence decisions, resolved clarifications, finalized findings, linked corrective actions for confirmed NCs and the form’s optional completion approval.

Findings retain facts, requirement, clause, source question, evidence, auditor and owner. Draft wording can be corrected by the responsible auditor or lead, retaining earlier facts. The designated auditee explicitly agrees or disputes a finding; disagreement requires an explanation. The lead confirms or withdraws it and records a reason for a withdrawal or disagreement resolution. A finding cannot be silently dropped by withdrawing or revising its source form. Reopening requires a reason and clears the current review/disposition while preserving history. Confirmed NCs have an idempotent Corrective Action link; observations/opportunities and withdrawn findings do not invent corrective actions. Auditee review is separate from an optional form approval.

Each contributing auditor saves an attributable summary and conclusion. The lead writes the report narrative and explicitly includes, merges or excludes every finding. Merge targets must be directly included findings with the same clause and classification, with an explanation. Only withdrawn findings may be excluded, with a reason. Decisions bind the reviewed finding revision; changed findings require another editorial review.

Report issue validates the completed forms, finding dispositions, corrective-action links, contributions and narrative. It freezes the audit header, all source form versions (including replaced/withdrawn ones), assignments, answers, assessments, evidence decisions, clarifications, findings and editorial decisions. The issued audit is read-only. A reasoned amendment creates a new draft/report version and preserves the issued version. Printing uses the same authorized report. Full report access requires access to all source forms; a partially shared auditor can prepare their own contribution without receiving hidden narrative, findings or source counts. Current evidence classification and access checks apply to historical snapshots too.

Planning opens the audit overview. Adding the first checklist explicitly copies the chosen template’s questions and questionnaire setting; future template edits do not alter that copy. Rescheduling records the old/new date and reason. A worked audit cannot be moved to another process through a plan patch; use the existing authorized structural-copy command. Lifecycle badges derive from stored work and cannot be set to Report issued through the programme menu.

## Boundary

This completes the audit workflow in the existing synthetic, browser-local preview. It does not add shared server persistence, verified identity/invitations, email delivery or production authorization. The normative backend specification remains responsible for transactions, organization isolation, verified identities, durable histories and concurrency enforcement. No deployment is authorized by this decision.
