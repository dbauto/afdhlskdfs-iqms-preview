## Purpose
Protect customer data and the ThryveMS service from loss, misuse and disruption, and meet our commitments in the Terms, the Data Processing Agreement and the Security Overview.

## Requirements
1. Information security objectives are set yearly and tracked: no cross-customer data exposure, critical vulnerabilities mitigated within 72 hours, quarterly restore test passed, all staff trained.
2. Risks are recorded in `security/risk-register.csv` with owner, rating and treatment, reviewed quarterly. Only the Managing Director may accept a High risk.
3. Security is part of every change: threat review for new features, security tests in CI, review of changes to sign-in, permissions, logging, support access and infrastructure.
4. The policies POL-02 to POL-15 apply and are reviewed yearly.
5. Staff complete security and privacy training when they join and every year, and sign a confidentiality undertaking.
6. Security incidents are reported at once to security@iqms.ph and handled under POL-09.

## Roles
- Managing Director: approves policies, accepts high risks, provides resources.
- Security owner: maintains the policies, risk register and controls register; reports monthly.
- Every staff member: follows the policies and reports incidents.

## Exceptions
Exceptions need a written reason, a compensating control, the risk owner’s approval and an end date no later than 6 months away. They are listed in `policies/exceptions.md` and reviewed monthly.

## Evidence
Approved policy set; training records; risk register; monthly security report.
