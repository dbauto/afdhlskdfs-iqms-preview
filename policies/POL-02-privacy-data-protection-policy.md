## Purpose
Comply with Republic Act No. 10173 (Data Privacy Act of 2012), its IRR and NPC issuances.

## Requirements
1. A Data Protection Officer is appointed in writing, published in the Privacy Notice and registered with the NPC where NPC Circular No. 2022-04 requires it.
2. A record of processing activities is kept in `privacy/processing-register.csv` (purpose, data, subjects, lawful basis, recipients, location, retention).
3. For customer data ThryveMS acts only on the customer’s documented instructions under the Data Processing Agreement.
4. New processing or major changes need a Privacy Impact Assessment (`policies/PIA-ThryveMS.md` template) before go-live.
5. Lawful basis is assessed per purpose; consent is not used where another basis applies.
6. Data subject requests to ThryveMS as controller are answered within 15 working days; requests about customer data are passed to the customer within 5 working days.
7. Transfers outside the Philippines happen only to subprocessors listed on the Subprocessors page under contracts with comparable protection.
8. Personal data breaches are handled under POL-09, including notice to customers within 24 hours of confirmation and support for NPC notification within 72 hours.
9. Privacy notices are reviewed when processing changes and at least yearly.

## Exceptions
Exceptions need a written reason, a compensating control, the risk owner’s approval and an end date no later than 6 months away. They are listed in `policies/exceptions.md` and reviewed monthly.

## Evidence
DPO appointment; NPC registration record; processing register; PIAs; request log; breach register.
