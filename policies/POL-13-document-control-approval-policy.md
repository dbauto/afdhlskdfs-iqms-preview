## Requirements
1. Approvals are tied to the exact revision and the external version reference that was reviewed.
2. Authors can’t approve their own revisions; approval doesn’t imply edit or publish rights.
3. At most one approval cycle runs per document at a time; changes requested start a new cycle.
4. Published revisions can’t be edited; corrections need a new revision.
5. Management review records lock when recorded; later changes need an amendment with a reason.
6. Customers remain responsible for their own document control procedure and for keeping file versions in their storage.

## Exceptions
Exceptions need a written reason, a compensating control, the risk owner’s approval and an end date no later than 6 months away. They are listed in `policies/exceptions.md` and reviewed monthly.

## Evidence
Workflow tests; approval history samples in UAT.
