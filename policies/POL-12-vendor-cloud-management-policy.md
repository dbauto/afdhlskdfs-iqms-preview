| Applies to | Suppliers that process data or run part of the service |

## Requirements
1. Before use, each vendor is assessed for security (independent reports such as SOC 2), data location, contract terms (data processing, breach notice, deletion) and exit options. Results in `privacy/vendor-register.csv`.
2. Subprocessors are listed on the Subprocessors page; customers get 30 days’ notice of changes.
3. Vendor access and costs are reviewed quarterly; security reports yearly.
4. Account ownership and recovery for each vendor rests with the company, not a person’s private account; at least two named administrators.
5. An exit plan exists for each critical vendor (Cloudflare, Neon, AWS, WorkOS).

## Exceptions
Exceptions need a written reason, a compensating control, the risk owner’s approval and an end date no later than 6 months away. They are listed in `policies/exceptions.md` and reviewed monthly.

## Evidence
Vendor register; assessments; contracts; exit plans.
