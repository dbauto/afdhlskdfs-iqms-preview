# POL-14 Acceptable Use and Device Security Policy (staff)

| | |
|---|---|
| Version | 1.0 draft |
| Owner | Security owner |
| Approved by | Managing Director, [Operator legal name] |
| Effective | On approval |
| Next review | 12 months after approval, and after any major incident or change |
| Applies to | iQMS staff and contractors and the devices they use for work |

## Requirements
1. Work devices have full-disk encryption, automatic updates, screen lock within 5 minutes and supported operating systems.
2. Password manager and MFA on all work accounts.
3. No customer data on personal devices, personal cloud storage or AI tools.
4. Lost or stolen devices are reported within 1 hour; sessions and tokens are revoked.
5. Staff follow the customer-facing Acceptable Use Policy where they use iQMS themselves.

## Exceptions
Exceptions need a written reason, a compensating control, the risk owner’s approval and an end date no later than 6 months away. They are listed in `policies/exceptions.md` and reviewed monthly.

## Evidence
Device checklist per staff member; incident reports.
