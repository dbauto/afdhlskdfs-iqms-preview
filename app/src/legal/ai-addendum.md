# AI Features Addendum

This addendum applies only if your company turns on an iQMS AI feature. All AI features are off by default.

## What is covered

**AI assistance for users (not yet available).** When offered, it will draft text such as audit questions or corrective-action descriptions, and summarise records the user can already see. It will only read records the user is allowed to see, will show which records it used, and can’t approve, publish, close, share, export or delete anything. A person reviews every draft.

**AI-assisted maintenance (internal to iQMS).** We may use an AI coding assistant to help fix software faults. It works on our source code and on synthetic test data only. It never receives your company’s records, has no access to production systems, and can’t release changes; a named iQMS engineer reviews and approves every release.

## Your data and AI

- We won’t send your company’s records to an AI provider unless your administrator turns on an AI feature for your workspace.
- Before we enable a provider, we add it to the Subprocessors page with 30 days’ notice, and we use only provider terms that don’t allow your data to be used to train their models.
- Your administrator chooses which classifications the feature may read. Highly confidential records are never sent.
- Prompts and outputs are kept only as long as needed to show the draft and its sources, then deleted with the record or within 30 days.

## Responsibility

AI output can be wrong. It is a draft until a person in your company reviews and accepts it. iQMS AI features don’t give legal, certification or professional advice.

You can turn AI features off at any time; everything else in iQMS keeps working.
