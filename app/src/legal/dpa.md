# Data Processing Agreement

This Data Processing Agreement ("DPA") is between the Customer named in the Order Form, as **personal information controller** ("Customer"), and **[Operator legal name]** ("ThryveMS"), as **personal information processor**. It forms part of the Terms of Service and is the outsourcing agreement required by sections 43 to 45 of the Implementing Rules and Regulations of Republic Act No. 10173 (Data Privacy Act of 2012) ("DPA Law").

## 1. Scope of processing

| Item | Details |
|---|---|
| Subject matter | Hosting and operating the Customer’s ThryveMS workspace |
| Duration | The subscription term, plus the exit and deletion periods in section 11 |
| Nature and purpose | Storing, organising, displaying, transmitting and deleting Customer Data so the Customer can run its quality management system; sending notifications; keeping backups and logs; providing support when the Customer grants access |
| Types of personal data | Names, work email addresses, job titles, process and role assignments, sign-in and session data, IP addresses, records and comments that Users write, activity logs. The Customer must not put sensitive personal information into the Service unless both parties agree in writing |
| Categories of data subjects | The Customer’s employees, contractors, auditors, consultants and other Users; individuals named in Customer records (for example customer or supplier contacts) |
| Location of processing | Singapore (database and backups); Cloudflare R2 with an Asia-Pacific location hint for uploaded Public and Internal files; Cloudflare’s edge network for request handling; United States for email-and-password sign-in through WorkOS. See the Subprocessors page |

## 2. Instructions

2.1 ThryveMS processes Customer personal data only on the Customer’s documented instructions. These Terms, this DPA, the Customer’s settings in the Service and its administrators’ actions are the instructions.

2.2 ThryveMS will tell the Customer at once if it believes an instruction breaks the DPA Law, and may pause that processing until the Customer confirms or changes it.

2.3 ThryveMS may process data otherwise only where Philippine law requires it, and will tell the Customer first unless the law forbids it.

## 3. Confidentiality

ThryveMS ensures that its staff and subcontractors who can access Customer personal data are bound by confidentiality obligations, are trained on data protection and security, and access data only as far as their work needs.

## 4. Security measures

4.1 ThryveMS keeps organisational, physical and technical security measures appropriate to the risk, in line with section 25 of the DPA Law and NPC Circular No. 2023-06, including:
- a separate database for each customer, with checks that block one customer’s requests from reaching another’s data;
- encryption in transit (TLS 1.2 or later) and at rest;
- role- and classification-based access in the application, with two-step sign-in required for administrators;
- staff access to the Customer workspace only through a time-limited support grant from the Customer (section 7), and logged emergency infrastructure access under section 7.4;
- tamper-evident activity logs, with daily checkpoints kept in write-once storage;
- backups with point-in-time restore for 30 days and encrypted nightly copies kept for 35 days;
- vulnerability management, dependency and secret scanning, and an independent penetration test before launch and yearly.

4.2 The Security Overview describes these measures in more detail. ThryveMS won’t materially reduce them during the term.

## 5. Subprocessors

5.1 The Customer authorises ThryveMS to use the subprocessors on the Subprocessors page.

5.2 ThryveMS will give at least 30 days’ notice (by email to the Customer’s administrators and on the Subprocessors page) before adding or replacing a subprocessor. The Customer may object on reasonable data protection grounds within that period. The parties will discuss in good faith; if no solution is found, the Customer may end the affected service and receive a refund of prepaid fees for the remaining term.

5.3 ThryveMS imposes data protection obligations on each subprocessor that are no less protective than this DPA and remains responsible for their performance.

## 6. Helping the Customer

6.1 **Data subject requests.** The Service lets the Customer access, correct, export, block and delete personal data. If ThryveMS receives a request directly, it will pass it to the Customer within 5 working days and won’t answer it except to say it was passed on.

6.2 **Assessments and the NPC.** ThryveMS will give reasonable information to help the Customer with privacy impact assessments, NPC registration and NPC enquiries about the Service.

## 7. Support access and infrastructure access

7.1 ThryveMS staff can open the Customer’s workspace only if a Customer administrator grants support access for a stated purpose and support ticket.

7.2 A grant lasts 1, 2 or 4 hours, can be renewed by the Customer, is read-only, and excludes Confidential and Highly confidential records. The Customer can end it at any time.

7.3 Every action during support access is recorded in the Customer’s activity log under the real name of the ThryveMS staff member.

7.4 Separately, a small number of named ThryveMS engineers have infrastructure access to keep the Service running and secure. They use it only for maintenance, incident response or recovery, never to read Customer Data for any other purpose. Each use is logged and reviewed monthly, and the Customer will be told if Customer personal data was viewed.

## 8. Personal data breaches

8.1 ThryveMS will notify the Customer’s administrators and Data Protection Officer **without undue delay and in any case within 24 hours** after confirming a personal data breach that affects Customer personal data, and will give an initial notice sooner if a breach is suspected and the Customer may need to act.

8.2 The notice will describe, as far as known: what happened, when, the data and number of people affected, likely consequences, measures taken or proposed, and a contact person. ThryveMS will add information as it becomes available.

8.3 ThryveMS will help the Customer meet its obligation under NPC Circular No. 16-03 to notify the NPC and affected individuals within 72 hours of knowledge, where notification is required. The Customer decides whether and how to notify. ThryveMS won’t notify the NPC or individuals about Customer data on the Customer’s behalf unless the Customer asks it to or the law requires it.

8.4 ThryveMS will keep records of breaches and security incidents affecting the Service for at least 5 years.

## 9. Audits and information

9.1 ThryveMS will make available the information needed to show it complies with this DPA, including its current security policies, a summary of its latest penetration test and its subprocessor list.

9.2 Once a year, or after a personal data breach affecting the Customer, the Customer may audit ThryveMS’s compliance with this DPA, at its own cost, on 30 days’ notice, during business hours, without access to other customers’ data and under confidentiality. ThryveMS may meet the request with a recent independent report covering the same controls.

## 10. Cross-border transfers

Customer personal data is processed in the locations in section 1. ThryveMS remains accountable under section 21 of the DPA Law for personal data transferred outside the Philippines and uses contracts with its subprocessors that require comparable protection.

## 11. Return and deletion

11.1 During the term the Customer can export its data at any time through the Service.

11.2 When the agreement ends, the Customer has 30 days to export its data. ThryveMS then deletes Customer Data from the live workspace within 30 days and from backups as they expire, no later than 35 days after that, unless Philippine law requires ThryveMS to keep part of it. ThryveMS will confirm deletion in writing on request.

11.3 Activity logs about ThryveMS’s own staff actions and security events may be kept for up to 1 year after the end of the agreement for security and legal purposes, after which they are deleted or anonymised.

## 12. Liability

Each party’s liability under this DPA is governed by the Terms of Service. Nothing in this DPA limits liability that cannot be limited under the DPA Law.

## 13. Order of precedence

If this DPA conflicts with the Terms of Service about personal data, this DPA prevails.
