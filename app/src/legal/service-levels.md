# Service Levels and Support

## 1. Availability

iQMS aims to be available **99.5% of each calendar month**, measured at the iQMS web address of your workspace.

Not counted: planned maintenance announced at least 3 working days ahead and done between 22:00 and 06:00 Philippine time (at most 4 hours a month), problems caused by your systems, your internet connection or Microsoft 365, suspension under the Terms, and events beyond our reasonable control.

**Service credits.** If monthly availability falls below the target, ask within 30 days and we credit your next invoice:

| Monthly availability | Credit (of that month’s fee) |
|---|---|
| Below 99.5% | 10% |
| Below 99.0% | 25% |
| Below 95.0% | 50% |

Credits are your only financial remedy for availability, and are capped at 50% of the month’s fee.

## 2. Recovery

| | Target |
|---|---|
| Data loss in a disaster (recovery point) | At most 24 hours; normally under 1 hour using point-in-time restore |
| Time to restore service after a disaster (recovery time) | At most 8 hours |

We test restoring a customer workspace every quarter and keep the results.

## 3. Support

**How to reach us.** In iQMS: Settings › Contact support. Email: support@iqms.ph. Hours: Monday to Friday, 09:00–18:00 Philippine time, excluding Philippine public holidays.

| Priority | Example | First response | Updates |
|---|---|---|---|
| Urgent | Nobody can sign in; data looks exposed | 1 hour, any time | Every 2 hours until resolved |
| High | A main function doesn’t work and there’s no workaround | 4 business hours | Daily |
| Normal | A function doesn’t work as expected but there’s a workaround | 1 business day | Every 3 business days |
| Question | How do I…? Ideas | 2 business days | When answered |

Security incidents and suspected data exposure are always treated as Urgent.

## 4. Support access to your workspace

We never open your workspace on our own. If we need to see what you see:
1. A support engineer asks, quoting the support report number.
2. Your Owner or Administrator grants access in Settings › Users and access, choosing 1, 2 or 4 hours.
3. Access is read-only and excludes Confidential and Highly confidential records.
4. You can end it at any time; it also ends on its own.
5. Every page opened is recorded in your activity log under the engineer’s real name.

## 5. Changes and releases

We release improvements regularly, to a test environment first, then a pilot customer, then everyone. Changes that need you to do something are announced at least 30 days ahead. Urgent security fixes may be applied at once.

## 6. Your responsibilities

Keep a named Owner and at least one Administrator active, keep your users’ access up to date, manage your SharePoint or OneDrive permissions and file versions, and tell us promptly about problems.
