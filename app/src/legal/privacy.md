# Privacy Notice

This notice explains how personal data is handled in ThryveMS, an online ISO 9001 quality management system run by **[Operator legal name]** ("ThryveMS", "we"). It follows Republic Act No. 10173, the Data Privacy Act of 2012, its Implementing Rules and Regulations, and issuances of the National Privacy Commission (NPC).

## 1. Who is responsible

There are two kinds of personal data in ThryveMS, with different people responsible:

| Personal data | Responsible (personal information controller) | ThryveMS’s role |
|---|---|---|
| What your company puts into its ThryveMS workspace: your name, work email, job title, role, the records you own, review or approve, comments, and the activity log | **Your company** | We process it for your company under a data processing agreement and only on its instructions |
| What ThryveMS collects to run its business: customer account contacts, billing contacts, support requests, sign-in security events, website enquiries and access requests | **ThryveMS** | We decide how it is used, as described here |

For questions about your company’s workspace data, contact your company’s Data Protection Officer first. You can also contact ours (section 10).

## 2. What we collect

**When you use a workspace**
- Identity and contact: name, work email, job title, the process you work in.
- Account and access: your role, permissions, sign-in method, two-step sign-in status, session times.
- Work records: documents you own (descriptions and links, not file contents), reviews and approvals, risks, actions, audits, comments and remarks you write.
- Activity log: who viewed, changed, approved or shared a record, and when, as ISO 9001 traceability evidence.
- Security data: sign-in attempts, IP address (stored as a one-way hash after 90 days), browser type, and security alerts.

**When you deal with ThryveMS directly**
- Access requests and enquiries: name, work email, company, message.
- Billing contacts: name, email, company details, invoices.
- Support reports: what you write, and technical details only if you tick "Include technical details" (app version, browser, screen size, your role, the page and recent error messages; never record contents or what you typed in forms).

We don’t ask for sensitive personal information (such as health, religion or government ID numbers). Please don’t put it in ThryveMS records or support reports.

## 3. Why we use it, and on what basis

| Purpose | Lawful basis under the Data Privacy Act |
|---|---|
| Running your company’s workspace: signing you in, showing what you can see and do, notifying you of work assigned to you | Your company’s contract with ThryveMS and its legitimate interest in running its quality system (your company is the controller) |
| Keeping the activity log for audits | Your company’s legitimate interest and its obligations under ISO 9001 certification |
| Keeping ThryveMS secure: detecting attacks, preventing fraud, investigating incidents | Our legitimate interest in protecting the service and its users |
| Answering support reports and access requests | Our legitimate interest; your request |
| Billing and accounting | Our contract with your company and our legal obligations (tax and accounting records) |
| Telling you about important service changes | Our contract with your company |

We don’t sell personal data, we don’t use it for advertising, and we don’t make decisions about you based solely on automated processing.

## 4. Who we share it with

- **Your company and the people it authorises**, within the limits of your roles and the record’s classification.
- **An outside ISO consultant**, only if your company approves their access. Consultants see Public and Internal records, Confidential records only when named on them, and never Highly confidential records.
- **ThryveMS support staff**, only during support access that your company’s administrator grants for a limited time, read-only and excluding Confidential and Highly confidential records.
- **Service providers (subprocessors)** that host and run ThryveMS, listed on the Subprocessors page with what they do and where.
- **Authorities**, when the law requires it. We will tell your company unless the law forbids it.

## 5. Where your data is kept

Workspace data is stored in **Singapore** (database and backups on Amazon Web Services, through Neon and Amazon S3). Requests pass through Cloudflare’s network, usually its Manila or Singapore data centres. If you sign in with email and password instead of Microsoft 365, your sign-in details are held by WorkOS in the **United States**.

These locations are outside the Philippines. Under section 21 of the Data Privacy Act we remain responsible for your data wherever it is processed, and our contracts with these providers require security measures equivalent to ours.

## 6. How long we keep it

| Data | Kept for |
|---|---|
| Workspace records and the activity log | As set by your company’s retention rules (Settings › Record retention), then deleted. Quality records you approved may be kept, with your name, for as long as your company keeps them as ISO evidence |
| Your account in a workspace | While your membership is active, then removed within 30 days of your company deactivating it, except where records must show who did what |
| Sign-in and security logs | 90 days in full, then up to 1 year with IP addresses hashed |
| Backups | Up to 35 days, then overwritten |
| Support reports | 12 months after closing |
| Billing records | For the period Philippine tax and accounting rules require |
| Access requests that are not approved | 90 days |

## 7. How we protect it

Every company gets its own database. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Access is limited by role and classification, two-step sign-in is required for administrators, and everything sensitive is logged. Our staff can’t see your company’s workspace without a time-limited grant from your administrator. The Security Overview has the details.

## 8. Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act you have the right to be informed, to object, to access, to correct, to have data erased or blocked, to data portability, to damages, and to file a complaint with the NPC.

- For workspace data, use **Settings › Privacy** in ThryveMS (download, correct or ask to delete your data) or contact your company’s DPO. Your company decides; we help it carry out the request.
- For data ThryveMS controls, email **privacy@iqms.ph**. We reply within 15 working days.

Some rights are limited by law, for example where a quality record must be kept as evidence or for legal claims. We will explain if a request can’t be fully met.

## 9. Cookies

ThryveMS uses only cookies needed to sign you in, keep the session secure and run the human check on sign-in pages. We don’t use advertising or tracking cookies. See the Cookie Notice.
