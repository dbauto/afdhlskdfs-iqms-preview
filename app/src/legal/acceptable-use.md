# Acceptable Use Policy

This policy applies to everyone who uses ThryveMS. Your company is responsible for making sure its users follow it.

## Do

- Use ThryveMS for your company’s quality management system and related work.
- Keep your sign-in details to yourself and use two-step sign-in.
- Classify records correctly. Mark records Confidential or Highly confidential when they need it, and keep personal details out of titles and descriptions.
- Link to documents in your company’s own storage rather than pasting their contents.
- Report suspected security problems to your administrator and to security@iqms.ph.

## Don’t

- Share your account, or sign in as someone else.
- Try to access workspaces, records or data you aren’t authorised to see, or probe, scan or test ThryveMS’s security without our written permission.
- Put sensitive personal information (for example health information or government ID numbers) into ThryveMS unless your company has agreed this with us in writing.
- Upload or link to malware, or content that is unlawful, defamatory, or infringes others’ rights.
- Use ThryveMS to send spam or unsolicited messages.
- Overload the service, for example with automated bulk requests, or work around usage limits.
- Copy, resell or reverse engineer ThryveMS, or use it to build a competing product.
- Use ThryveMS in a way that breaks Philippine law, including the Data Privacy Act of 2012 and the Cybercrime Prevention Act of 2012.

## What happens if the policy is broken

We may suspend the account or feature involved to protect the service and other customers, as described in the Terms of Service. We’ll tell your administrator and, where practical, give a chance to fix the problem first.

Questions: legal@iqms.ph.
